The brief

I founded and ran Lagoon during my internship at Decommune Bangkok. The challenge: build a promoter brand that speaks to Gen Z indie music fans.

I named it Lagoon because I wanted every show to feel like a sanctuary: a warm welcome from the staff, the decoration, even the scent of the room.

What I did

  • Pitched the concept solo to a venue owner, secured him as investor, and built the founding team
  • Produced 4 live shows at ARTSPACE @Bantadthong with 9 acts: Yew, Television Off, Landokmai, Tofu, AYLA'S, The White Haircut, SOK, Yented and Cornboi
  • Owned the budget, P&L tracking, production timeline and artist liaison
  • Reclaimed-plastic set design became the brand's visual signature

The result

  • Sold out a 400-capacity show
  • 200,000–300,000 THB ticket revenue, tickets 600–850 THB via Ticketmelon
  • ~200 attendance at the other dates
  • Partner creator reel reached 407K views organically
